## Sensitive editing and preservation

Native replacement removes the selected text from the exported PDF content streams. It is not a forensic document sanitization service: other occurrences, metadata, form fields, annotations, attachments, OCR image pixels, and local undo/original copies may still contain the same information. Always inspect the final export for the intended use. History and the original are intentionally retained in the local workspace until close/delete or retention cleanup. File deletion is not secure erasure.

Redaction is rectangular and can remove overlapping glyphs. Overlapping spans, rotated/vertical text, transparency groups, unusual encodings, forms, tagged PDFs, clipping and complex backgrounds require review. Fallback fonts can change metrics. Automatic fitting can shrink text to 4 pt; smaller or strict overflowing replacements are rejected. Moving a span does not reflow surrounding text. Page crop changes visibility, not removal of content outside the crop; do not use cropping to redact secrets.

Modification invalidates most digital signatures. The source is preserved, but exported PDFs are rewritten and do not retain valid cryptographic signatures. Signatures are never fabricated. OCR reconstruction is not recovery of original editable text.

## Production work remaining

A larger customer-document corpus of Arabic, signed, embedded-font, rotated, table-heavy and scanned documents; interactive Windows 11 Pro installation; dependency/security audit; robust native-memory/disk budgets and hostile-file sandboxing; pixel-region checks for complex documents; improved conversion reconstruction; paragraph editing, selectable viewer text, rich annotation management and fine-grained job recovery are still required before calling this production complete.

## Scanned fields and desktop update (0.3.0)

Selected raster regions can be replaced independently of the OCR layer. An original-area preview and English/Arabic regional OCR (line, block or numbers/date mode; 300–600 DPI) support manual correction before applying. Simple RGB/gray raster samples and compatible masks are changed locally, preserving other decoded samples; complex images use MuPDF pixel redaction. Layered/compressed masks can show slight rendering differences outside the selected field after re-encoding. Exact font/background/texture matching and paragraph reflow are not guaranteed.

A persistent baseline enables Discard all changes even after revision pruning. Windows desktop launch uses pythonw and a no-console background service; Settings can stop it. Installation windows and manual developer launchers still use consoles.
